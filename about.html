About this site

A focused, independent guide to one part of the Starbucks menu: lunch.

What this site is

Starbucks Lunch Menu is an independent, informational website built to help people quickly compare Starbucks lunch sandwiches, pockets, and protein boxes in one place — with photos, descriptions, reference prices, and calorie counts side by side.

What this site is not

This is not the official Starbucks website, and it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Starbucks Corporation. Starbucks® is a registered trademark of Starbucks Corporation. For official, up-to-date menu information, ordering, and store-specific pricing, visit starbucks.com or the Starbucks app.

How the information is put together

  • Menu items are based on publicly available Starbucks lunch offerings.
  • Prices are shown as reference or estimated figures, since actual pricing varies by store, city, and region.
  • Calorie figures reflect standard published nutrition information rather than numbers pulled from any single location.
  • Photos are added by the site owner and are not sourced from Starbucks' own marketing materials.

Questions or corrections

Menus, prices, and availability change over time. If something here looks out of date, the best source of truth is always your local Starbucks store or the official Starbucks app.
