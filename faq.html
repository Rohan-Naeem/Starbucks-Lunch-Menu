Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about Starbucks lunch hours, pricing, calories, and customization. Since menus and prices vary by store, treat these as general guidance.

What time does Starbucks serve lunch?

Most Starbucks locations offer lunch sandwiches, pockets, and protein boxes throughout the day, generally from mid-morning through the evening. Exact timing depends on the store, so it's worth checking with your local location or the Starbucks app.

What foods are available on the Starbucks lunch menu?

The lunch menu generally includes sandwiches such as grilled cheese, ham and Swiss, and turkey pesto, plus pockets like falafel and jalapeño chicken, and protein boxes built around eggs, cheese, or salami.

How much does Starbucks lunch cost?

Lunch items generally range from around $5 for a pocket to around $9 for a larger sandwich, though exact prices vary by store location and region.

How many calories are in Starbucks lunch items?

Calories across the lunch menu typically range from about 90 for a small side to roughly 530 for a fuller protein box or sandwich, depending on the item and any customization.

Are Starbucks lunch prices the same everywhere?

No. Prices can vary by city, state, and individual store, and may change over time, so the prices listed here should be treated as general reference points rather than exact current pricing.

Can Starbucks lunch items be customized?

Some lunch items can be adjusted, such as adding a side like avocado spread, though sandwiches and protein boxes generally come pre-packaged with a fixed set of ingredients.

Which Starbucks lunch items are lower in calories?

The Jalapeño Chicken Pocket and Spicy Falafel Pocket are among the lower-calorie options, generally sitting under 250 calories, with the Tomato & Mozzarella on Focaccia also coming in under 400.
